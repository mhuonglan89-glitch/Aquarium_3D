Downloaded aquarium fish models (CC0):

- BarramundiFish.glb — Microsoft model from Khronos glTF Sample Assets; CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/BarramundiFish
- Pufferfish.glb — original Innerscene pufferfish model; CC0 public domain.
  https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/pufferfish-inflated-bd261bf7
- BlueTang.glb — Polyfork Blue Tang model. Attribution is included below for
  redistribution with the project; CC BY 4.0.
  https://polyfork.dev/asset/blue-tang-base
  Credit: Blue tang by Polyfork (https://polyfork.dev), CC BY 4.0.
- LeafySeadragon.glb — leafy seadragon, 3DAssets.dev Ocean Giants pack; CC0 1.0.
  https://3dassets.dev/assets/ocean-giants-kit-leafy-seadragon-cfed69e6
- Seahorse.glb — big-belly seahorse, 3DAssets.dev Ocean Giants pack; CC0 1.0.
  https://3dassets.dev/assets/ocean-giants-kit-big-belly-seahorse-684786b9
- JellyfishBloom.glb — five jellyfish in a floating group, 3DAssets.dev Ocean
  Giants pack; CC0 1.0.
  https://3dassets.dev/assets/ocean-giants-kit-jellyfish-bloom-7379ead7

The application reads GLB base-color textures and vertex colors from the models.
The fish swim across the tank, while the jellyfish group drifts gently in mid-water.

- MahiMahi.glb — mahi mahi swimming study from 3DAssets.dev Ocean Animals pack; CC0 1.0, no attribution required.
  https://3dassets.dev/assets/open-ocean-and-deep-sea-life-mahi-mahi-65ecf8f3
  Static smooth-shaded model; the aquarium animates its position across a separate lane.
